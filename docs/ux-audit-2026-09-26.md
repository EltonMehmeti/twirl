# Vesha UX audit — 26 Sep 2026

Method: every flow walked end to end against the running app (53 page views plus a repeat of the
walk-in conflict), the way a browser would: first visit, salon onboarding with mistakes, individual
who skips the item, renter booking, owner handling the request, walk-in conflict and override,
phone login, wrong addresses. Every page screenshotted at desktop and phone width.
Screenshots and HTML snapshots: `~/vesha-audit/`.

## A. Broken — fix before anyone real uses it

| # | Problem | Where | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | Providers who sign up by phone never hear about new requests. Owner alerts go by email only, and phone sign-ups have no email. The request then auto-cancels after 24 h. | notifications | Needs the SMS/WhatsApp provider decision. Interim: optional email at onboarding, plus a clear "check your dashboard" line on the done page |
| A2 | Renters landing on the home page cannot find a single dress. It only offers "List your clothes" and "Shop login". | `/` | Home lists published shops and newest dresses, filtered by city |
| A3 | A wrong link shows raw `{"detail":"Not Found"}`. | any 404 | Branded 404 and 403 pages with a way back |
| A4 | The request form allows 5 attempts per hour per IP, and failed attempts count. Kosovo mobile carriers put many phones behind one IP, and my own third try was blocked. | dress page | Count only successful requests, key by phone + IP, raise the limit |
| A5 | Dress cards in the dashboard print `<built-in method items of dict…>` instead of the piece count. The bug predates the restyle. | `/shop/styles` | Rename the field; add a render test |
| A6 | Half the settings labels are English ("Shop name", "Pickup: days before the event", "WhatsApp number"). The translation check can't see labels passed into the field helper. | `/shop/settings` | Wrap labels in `_()` at the call site |
| A7 | System reasons shown to shops are English: "item taken in store: …", "no reply within SLA". | booking history | Store a code, translate on display |

## B. Confusing — the user has to stop and think

| # | Problem | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| B1 | After choosing a date, the renter sees "38: taken", but the size dropdown still offers 38 and the request then fails. | Replace the dropdown with size chips that come from the availability check; taken sizes are disabled |
| B2 | Two different dress editors. Onboarding: photos → category → size chips → price and dates. Dashboard "Add a dress": name, occasions, colour, a manual table of physical items, no category. | One editor: "Add a dress" reuses the onboarding item flow; the item table moves under an "advanced" section for salons |
| B3 | An unfinished onboarding item shows in the dress list as "Veshje e re · 0 € · hidden" and opens the old editor, not the resume flow. | Drafts show a "Finish" badge that opens `/listo/veshja` |
| B4 | Individuals get the salon dashboard: "New walk-in", "Shop settings", cleaning and prep days, a per-item calendar grid. | Individual mode: Today (requests and handovers), My items, Profile. Hide walk-in, rules and the grid |
| B5 | On a request, the decline reason box sits between Accept and Decline, so it looks required for both. | Accept is the main button; Decline opens its reason field when tapped |
| B6 | An at-risk booking with no free alternative only offers "Cancel". Nothing says what to do. | Show "No other free dress — call the customer" with call and WhatsApp buttons |
| B7 | Phone numbers appear as `+38345138900`. | Show `045 138 900`; tap to call, WhatsApp button next to it |
| B8 | The week calendar is unusable on a phone: one or two days fit on screen. | On phones, an agenda list (day by day: pickups, returns, blocks); the grid stays on tablet and desktop |
| B9 | A new shop's dashboard is six empty "0" boxes. | Empty lanes collapse; a getting-started checklist instead: share your link, add more dresses, write your terms |
| B10 | After publishing there is no way to share the listing: no copy link, no WhatsApp share, no Instagram-bio link. The spec's go-to-market depends on exactly this. | Share block on the done page and dashboard: copy link, WhatsApp, QR poster |
| B11 | The renter's confirmation can't be found again: no SMS, no email, no link to keep. | "Save this page" hint, add-to-calendar file, and the link in the WhatsApp message to the shop |
| B12 | The confirmation doesn't say what to bring or what happens next. | Next steps: wait for the confirmation, bring ID and deposit as per the shop's terms, pickup date |
| B13 | Phone login for an unknown number shows the code screen and no code ever arrives. This is deliberate, against enumeration, but confusing. | Line on the code screen: "If this number has a Vesha account you'll get a code. New? List your clothes" |
| B14 | "Klient i ri në dyqan" (walk-in) is jargon, and most entries will be phone bookings. | Rename to "Shto rezervim" (Add booking) |

## C. Polish

- C1. The onboarding busy-dates calendar starts at the 1st of the month, so most of the first month is disabled past days. Use a rolling six-week window from today.
- C2. The salon opening-hours row wraps badly on phones (the closing time drops to its own line).
- C3. On phones the dress gallery fills the whole screen, and the price and booking form are far below. Use a shorter gallery plus a sticky "Rezervo" bar.
- C4. The edit form shows price as `70.00`; the photo upload is an unstyled "Browse… No files selected" input.
- C5. The long override question on the walk-in form is set in small uppercase mono and is hard to read.
- C6. The admin console is English and off-brand. Acceptable, since only you use it.
- C7. The confirmation page has no `<h1>`, which is bad for screen readers.

## What works well

- The onboarding flow is clear, step by step, with good inline errors that mark every field at once.
- The walk-in conflict screen shows exactly which booking clashes and asks for a reason before overriding. The at-risk flag and history work.
- Availability gives an instant "free/taken per size" answer; the too-soon date message is clear.
- The booking history timeline makes it obvious what happened and when.

## Suggested order

1. **A1–A7**: small, except A1, which needs the SMS/WhatsApp provider decision.
2. **B1, B2 + B3, B5–B7, B10**: the renter's booking and the owner's daily loop.
3. **B4, B8, B9, B11–B14**: individual mode, phone calendar, onboarding polish.
4. **C**: polish.
